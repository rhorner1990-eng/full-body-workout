Progression: Build towards the top of each rep range. When you hit the top of the range on every set with good technique, increase the load slightly. Aim to keep 1–2 reps in reserve on most sets.
Technique note: Behind-the-neck presses require good shoulder mobility and may irritate the shoulder. Use only if comfortable and controlled; a standard overhead press is a sensible alternative. Stop if you feel pain or pinching.
Warm-up: Allow around 5 minutes for a general warm-up and lighter preparation sets. Keep transitions purposeful to stay within 60 minutes.
